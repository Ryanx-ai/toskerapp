# Tosker Vibeguide

Tosker should feel playful, social, warm, crafted, and alive—modern American technology with an editorial streak. It is utility software with a world, not sterile SaaS.

## Principles

- **Fun, not childish.** Friendly language and bright interactive energy without becoming children’s software.
- **Software with a world.** Identity and future illustration should feel native to the product environment.
- **Editorial × playful.** Mermaid creates expressive moments; Montserrat keeps controls and content lucid.
- **Dark, but not gamer-only.** Warm dark foundations must welcome trips, weddings, workshops, students, creators, and gamers.
- **Illustrations as product environment.** Subtle motifs can make rooms feel inhabited while utility stays primary.
- **Personality without clutter.** Motion is brief, accents deliberate, and whitespace protective.

## Conceptual lessons

- **Padlet:** friendly utility, expressive empty states, playful illustration, human personality.
- **Discord:** social spaces should feel inhabited, with clear places, people, and activity.
- **WhatsApp:** conversation stays obvious while environmental illustration adds warmth.
- **LINE:** characters may become native to communication culture.
- **FigJam:** people and shared interactive objects coexist in one collaborative environment.

These are lessons, not templates. Tosker must not copy their branding, layouts, illustrations, or interaction patterns.

## Foundation

Tosker Black `#080D10`, Tosker Ivory `#F4EFE6`, Squirrel Gold `#C89C5D`, Action Pink `#FF4F91`, and Action Yellow `#FFD84D` are provisional semantic tokens. Pink and yellow punctuate the experience rather than flood it.
