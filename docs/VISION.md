# Tosker Vision

Tosker is a shared digital room that becomes whatever the people inside it need.

## Product evolution

**Event-first → Room-first → Programmable rooms**

The first wedge is event-first: create a room, share a link, and give a gathering one place for announcements, conversation, people, and lightweight planning. Joining should eventually be as low-friction as opening a link.

The underlying primitive is always the **Realm**. Trips, gaming groups, hackathons, weddings, workshops, and communities should become configurations of the same shared-room model rather than separate products.

Longer term, a Realm can contain contextual, persistent shared objects—maps, itineraries, polls, brackets, canvases, and purpose-built tools. The governing principle is:

> Apps extend the Realm. Apps do not own the Realm.

Milestone 1 implements only the visual shell and information architecture needed to test whether people want a Tosker for the things they do together.
